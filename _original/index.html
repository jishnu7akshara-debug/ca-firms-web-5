FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do you work with startups and small businesses?

More questions

What is the difference between a CA and a CS?

A Chartered Accountant (ICAI) specialises in accounting, audit and taxation. A Company Secretary (ICSI) specialises in corporate law, governance and ROC compliance. We offer both under one roof.

Which tax regime should I choose?

It depends on your income and deductions. We compute your tax under both regimes and recommend the cheaper option before filing.

When is GST registration mandatory?

Mainly when turnover crosses the prescribed threshold, for inter-state supplies of goods or services, or for e-commerce selling. Thresholds vary, so confirm with us.

How long does company incorporation take?

Usually 7 to 15 working days once documents and name approval are in order.

What if I missed a filing deadline?

File as soon as possible. Late fees and interest grow with delay, and we can also help with condonation or rectification where available.

Can you work with my existing accounting software?

Yes. We work with Tally, Zoho Books, QuickBooks and Excel, and can migrate you to cloud software if you wish.

How do I share documents securely?

Through our encrypted document portal or a secure shared folder. We advise against sending sensitive IDs over chat.
